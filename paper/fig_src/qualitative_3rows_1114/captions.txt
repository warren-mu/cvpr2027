FIGURE A · Three matched observation sequences (01_cases)
Three Look episodes from non-held-out iTHOR training scenes. At the selection step, Qwen2.5-VL-3B, -7B, and -32B received the same ordered five images within an episode. The obs_trigger controller turned left, returned to the starting heading, turned right, then returned again. The first four actions were scripted. The anchor was visible only on the rightward view (step 3, green corners); the final choice was made by each model at step 4 from the full five-frame history. The models selected different targets for the box and bread, and all three selected the wrong target for the bottle. The correctness column uses the nearest candidate in each world's original 3D geometry.

FIGURE B · Model rows for the same episode (02_models)
The three Qwen2.5-VL models saw the identical five frames in a Look–Flip episode in FloorPlan203. The box appeared in the rightward view and was out of view again at selection. The 3B model selected the book (#1), the correct target. The 7B and 32B models selected the bowl (#2). The repeated frames in each row are the recorded views aligned to the same camera heading.
