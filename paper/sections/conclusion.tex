\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
All experiments use a single simulator, discrete viewpoints, single-object edits, and a 3B-parameter policy; we do not evaluate on real robots.
World pairs require a simulator that can restore and edit states deterministically and expose instance masks, which limits where the method applies directly.
The variance argument for pairing holds for a balanced prior with one rollout per world; for other priors the benefit must be established empirically.
